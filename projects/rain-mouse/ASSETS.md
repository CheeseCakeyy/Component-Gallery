# Asset provenance

`assets/original.png`: user-supplied photograph, copied without modification.

`assets/paper.png`: built-in image generation edit. Prompt: "Edit this image into a completely blank photographed paper background plate. Remove ALL remaining black ink drawing: remove the cloud from the upper area and remove all the rocks and ground marks from the bottom area. Inpaint with the same naturally mottled light gray paper. Preserve existing gray paper tone and grain, framing, proportions, page corners, and the dark vertical outside-page strip at right exactly. No drawings or marks remain anywhere. No text, no objects, only the original blank sheet. Keep the same composition and proportions."

Preparation used an initial background edit removing rain, mouse, and plant, followed by the blank-paper edit above. Visible mouse, plant, cloud, and rocks are clipped directly from original.png in the composition. No replacement subjects were generated.

`assets/gsap.min.js`: GSAP 3.14.2, installed from npm and copied locally for offline playback.
